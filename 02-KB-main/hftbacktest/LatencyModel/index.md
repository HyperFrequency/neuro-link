# LatencyModel/

`LatencyModel` trait is applied by the `OrderBus<LM>` to compute
`exch_arrival_ts` from outbound orders and `local_arrival_ts` from
inbound acks/fills. Feed latency (the gap between `exch_ts` and `local_ts`
on market events) is modeled separately via `FeedLatencyAdjustment`.

## Leaves

- [[ConstantLatency]] — fixed ns `entry`/`response`; simple baseline.
- [[IntpOrderLatency]] — interpolates historical
  `(req_ts, exch_ts, resp_ts)` tuples from one or more NPZ files;
  captures intraday variation.
- [[FeedLatencyAdjustment]] — preprocessor that shifts `local_ts` by a
  constant or data-driven offset to simulate a different collection
  site.

## NPZ schema for `IntpOrderLatency`

Three int64 columns: `req_ts` (ns when strategy called submit),
`exch_ts` (ns when exchange received), `resp_ts` (ns when the ack
arrived back locally). Generated by recording every order round-trip
during a live run; replayable in a backtest to calibrate against the
observed day.
